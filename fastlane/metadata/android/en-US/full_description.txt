Klockin is a time-tracking companion for [Kimai](https://www.kimai.org/), the open-source time-tracking server.

Start and stop timesheets with a single tap — even while offline. Klockin stores your entries locally and syncs them with your Kimai server automatically once a connection is available.

Features:
- Start and stop new timesheets to track time, with full offline support
- Browse and manage activities, customers, and projects
- Review your recorded timesheets
- Log in quickly by scanning a QR code
- Home-screen widget showing today's worked duration, plus a one-tap toggle for the active timesheet
- Keep a local record of your data and export a full backup at any time

Klockin is free and open-source software, licensed under the GNU GPL v3.
